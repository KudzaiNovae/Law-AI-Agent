# OneGov AI — 2-Minute Expo Video Script

**Format:** screen recording of onegovai.app with voiceover. Optional: open on camera for the first 15 seconds, then cut to screen.
**Pace:** ~140 words/min voiceover = ~280 words total. Rehearse once with a timer.

---

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem (show: scrolling a scanned gazette PDF, then cut to onegovai.app homepage)

> Zimbabwe's laws are scattered across dozens of websites, buried in scanned PDFs, and written in language most people can't interpret. A lawyer costs more than most citizens can afford. So millions simply don't know their rights or their obligations. We built the solution.

## 0:20–0:35 — What it is (show: homepage, hover the stats line)

> OneGov AI is Zimbabwe's first AI legal assistant. Over fifteen thousand laws and nearly five thousand court judgments — the Constitution, Acts, Statutory Instruments, gazettes — in one place, answering questions in plain language.

## 0:35–1:20 — Live demo (show: type a real question, let the answer stream)

Suggested question: **"What documents do I need to register a private company?"**

> Watch. I ask in plain language, like I'd ask a person. Changamire, our assistant, searches the actual law and streams back an answer in seconds — with citations. Every answer is grounded in retrieved official documents, so it cannot make things up. I click a citation... and there's the original gazetted document.

(While it streams, click a citation, then briefly show the Case Law page and filter by court.)

> There's also a full case-law library — four thousand nine hundred judgments, searchable by court and year.

## 1:20–1:45 — Traction and how it sustains (show: homepage again, or a slide with the numbers)

> We launched in July. Since then: over two thousand seven hundred users across all ten provinces, our first paying subscribers, zero marketing spend. Free for citizens, subscriptions for professionals, and an API for institutions. The corpus updates itself every week when new gazettes are published.

## 1:45–2:00 — Team and close (on camera if possible, or logo end-card)

> We are Kudzai Zhuwaki and Presson Zhou of Longacre Technologies, a registered Zimbabwean company. Local innovation, built on Zimbabwean law, for every Zimbabwean. Try it now — onegovai dot app.

**End card (3 sec):** logo + "onegovai.app" + "Longacre Technologies (Private) Limited" (use brand/onegov_banner.png)

## Recording checklist

1. Recorder: OBS Studio or `SimpleScreenRecorder` (Ubuntu), 1920×1080, 30fps.
2. Browser: clean profile, hide bookmarks bar, 100% zoom, dark theme looks great on camera.
3. Pre-warm the site: ask one question first so the demo answer is fast, then record a fresh question.
4. Audio: record voiceover separately in a quiet room (phone voice memo works), then overlay in Kdenlive/CapCut — cleaner than talking while driving the mouse.
5. Keep the answer streaming shot at least 10 seconds — it's the money shot.
6. Export MP4 (H.264). Check final length is under 2:00.
