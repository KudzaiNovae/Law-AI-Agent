Urgent Chamber Application

Z C NCUBE for the applicant

R T HOVE for the respondent


 

MOYO J: This is an urgent application wherein the application seeks to stop an eviction and the interim relief is couched as follows

“That the Respondents be and are hereby interdicted for further execution of the wright to ejectment issued in case number HCBC 2513/23”

That an urgent application was filed by the applicant prior to this one on 9 March 2026, and it was struck off the roll on the assumption that eviction had already taken place. That eviction commenced on 11 March and it was only executed partially as applicant remains in occupation and has secured within the premises with a padlock. The date for execution on the attached Notice of removal is the 10/03/26.

The 1st Respondent apposed the application and raised preliminary objectives

  1. That the certificate of urgency is irregular in that whilst the eviction took place on 10 March, the legal practitioner attesting to urgency did so on 19 March 2026 long after the eviction had occurred and the urgency no longer existing.

That the application sought to stay an execution that had already happened and that therefore there is no urgency.

That applicant has approached the court with dirty hands in that they claim to be in occupation after being evicted.

I find that the certificate of urgency is irregular and defective and that for that reason alone, the matter ought not be placed on the urgent roll. It cannot be urgent on the basis of an irregular certificate of urgency. The certificate is irregular in that it seeks to render urgent on 19 March an eviction that already occurred on 10 March. The legal practitioner attesting to urgency did so without applying their mind to the matter and the events at hand. This is clear even from applicant’s own affidavit, where applicant claims partial eviction yet the sheriff clearly gave a return of service on 11 March attesting to the eviction having been duly executed.

For that reason again, there can be no urgency in a bid to stay execution that has already been affected.

The matter is thus not urgent and is accordingly struck off the roll with costs.
