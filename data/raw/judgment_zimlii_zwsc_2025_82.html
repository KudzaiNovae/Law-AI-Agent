Traditional Medical Practitioners council v Joyce Guhwa [2025] ZWSC 82 (6 March 2025)

Court: Supreme Court of Zimbabwe. Judgment date: 6 March 2025.

Held: Failure to hold a disciplinary hearing within 14 working days renders the proceedings null and attracts reinstatement.
