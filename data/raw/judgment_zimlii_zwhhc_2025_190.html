KAMBA TRAVEL AND TOURISM (PVT) LTD v SIBANDA (190 of 2025) [2025] ZWHHC 190 (11 February 2025)

Court: Harare High Court. Judgment date: 11 February 2025.

Held: An acknowledgement of debt was upheld as valid and not vitiated by duress or misrepresentation; appellant’s claim succeeded.

Flynote (issues and statutes considered): Contract law — Acknowledgement of debt — validity — caveat subscriptor principle; Duress and economic duress — requisites and threshold to vitiate consent; Liquid document — enforceability irrespective of ancillary events; Burden of proof when alleging misrepresentation or undue influence.
