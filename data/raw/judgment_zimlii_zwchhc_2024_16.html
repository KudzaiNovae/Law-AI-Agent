S v Katizamhuka (16 of 2024) [2024] ZWCHHC 16 (23 February 2024)

Court: Chinhoyi High Court. Judgment date: 23 February 2024.

Held: A sentencing error failing to apply a statutory minimum for pangolin possession was identified, but re-sentencing was refused to protect finality and avoid prejudice.

Flynote (issues and statutes considered): Criminal law — Parks and Wildlife Act — possession of specially protected animal — mandatory minimum sentence under s128(b) — effect of Statutory Instrument 71/20 — sentencing procedure and requirement to address special circumstances — review remedies and finality of litigation
