MUGUZA v MONTFORD INVESTMENTS (PRIVATE) LIMITED (142 of 2025) [2025] ZWHHC 142 (4 March 2025)

Court: Harare High Court. Judgment date: 4 March 2025.

Held: COVID‑19 did not constitute a statutory interruption of prescription; lease arrears claim prescribed and dismissed.

Flynote (issues and statutes considered): Prescription — Prescription Act (s 15(d), s 14(2), ss 18–19) — statutory grounds required for interruption of prescription — COVID‑19 pandemic and Practice Directions — judicial notice permitted but cannot create statutory interruption — lease arrears prescribed.
