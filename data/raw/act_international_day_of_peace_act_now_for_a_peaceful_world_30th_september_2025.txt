INTERNATIONAL DAY OF PEACE
[30th September 2025]
Act Now for a Peaceful World
The 21st September is commemorated annually as International Day of Peace, after it was designated as such by the General Assembly of the United Nations in 2001.  This year’s theme is:
Act now for a peaceful world.
That injunction seems more urgent than ever, because seldom in recent years has peace been more threatened and war on a global scale appeared more imminent.  Wars are raging on the African, European and Asian continents and at least some of them – the war in Ukraine in particular – are liable to become more widespread, dragging in more and more countries.  Even here in Zimbabwe peace is fragile, with polarised politics exacerbating the problems facing the country and making them more difficult to resolve peacefully.
The United Nations urges ordinary people to act for a peaceful world by talking about the need for understanding, non-violence and disarmament and by trying to resolve daily problems and disputes peacefully and amicably.  In Zimbabwe we must try to resolve political disputes according to the letter and spirit of our Constitution, remembering the principles it lays down in section 3:
·recognition of the inherent dignity and worth of each human being
·a multi-party democratic political system and respect for the rights of all political parties – which means all parties must be allowed to have their say, even if what they say is unpopular
·fostering of national unity while paying due regard to our diversity, and
·respect for vested rights.
The Constitution points Zimbabwe in the direction of peace.  If we want peace we must abide by the Constitution and its principles.
We can also pray for peace:
"Give peace in our time, O Lord; for it is you, Lord, only, that make us dwell in safety."
