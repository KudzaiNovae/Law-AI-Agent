Makosa v Estate Late Felix Chasi (Represented by Francis Chasi, the Executor) (8 of 2024) [2024] ZWCHHC 2 (8 February 2024)

Court: Chinhoyi High Court. Judgment date: 8 February 2024.

Held: Unjust enrichment award for improvements despite illegal sale; in pari delicto relaxed and US$18,000 granted to the plaintiff.

Flynote (issues and statutes considered): Unjust enrichment — compensation for improvements to immovable property — illegal sale and in pari delicto doctrine — relaxation of in pari delicto on equity grounds — valuation (forced sale v market value) — rental claims arising from illegal contracts — failure to prove prescription and dependants’ losses
