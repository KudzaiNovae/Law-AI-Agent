Runganga v Nyamangara (1 of 2024) [2024] ZWCHHC 1 (2 January 2024)

Court: Chinhoyi High Court. Judgment date: 2 January 2024.

Held: Community court can determine family occupation disputes; council registration and levies do not prove permanent residency or ownership.

Flynote (issues and statutes considered): Community court jurisdiction — distinction between disputes over allocation/ownership and disputes over occupation — Customary Law and Local Courts Act s16(1)(g) — Traditional Leaders Act s5 — payment of council levies/registration not proof of permanent residency or ownership — stare decisis (Chihoro v Rusere)

Extract: [...] The court a quo was bound by the decision of the superior court . ... The court made its decision based on the jurisdiction of the Community Court . [...]
