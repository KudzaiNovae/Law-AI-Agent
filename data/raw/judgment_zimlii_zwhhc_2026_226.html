S v Saungweme and Another (977 of 2026; HCHCR977/26) [2026] ZWHHC 226 (25 February 2026)

Court: Harare High Court. Judgment date: 25 February 2026.

Held: Late non-compliance with statutory disclosure timelines is curable; court may order service and grant time to amend defence to protect fair trial.
