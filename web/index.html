Common questions

Is OneGov legal?

Yes. Zimbabwe's Constitution, Acts, statutory instruments and Government Gazettes are public documents that every citizen is entitled to read. OneGov is an independent public-information service that makes those official texts easier to search and understand, the same way a library or a legal-resources website does.

Is this legal advice?

No. OneGov explains what the gazetted texts say and cites the exact sections so you can verify everything yourself. For a decision about your specific situation, consult a registered legal practitioner.

Do you cover court judgments?

Yes. OneGov indexes judgments from the Constitutional Court, Supreme Court, High Court, Labour Court and more, sourced from the Judicial Service Commission and ZimLII. You can search case law by party name, citation or court, and the AI can discuss how the courts have interpreted a provision.

How current are the documents?

Records are updated regularly from official sources, and each consolidated text shows its "as at" date so you always know the vintage you are reading. Official government sites remain the authoritative source.

What am I paying for?

You are paying for the AI assistant, never for the law itself: every official document on OneGov is free for everyone to browse, read and download. Paid passes are one-off 30-day passes (no auto-renewal) paid via Paynow: EcoCash, OneMoney or card. Plus removes the daily question limit and the ads and adds monthly PDF/Word exports of your answers and amendment watches. Pro makes exports unlimited, answers questions with deeper research and priority, and adds a one-click ZIP archive tool and a developer API; every document remains free to read and download individually. Payments go toward the hosting and running costs that keep OneGov AI online.

What does "Beta" mean?

OneGov is in beta: we are actively improving answers, coverage and features. Answers can occasionally be imperfect; every answer cites its sections precisely so you can check the original text right beside it.
